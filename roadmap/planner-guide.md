# Roadmap Planner Guide

## Purpose

The planner separates long-term direction from short-term execution:

1. **Visual roadmap** - remembers the major phases from August 2026 to December 2027.
2. **Annual planner** - defines what each year should produce from 2026 to 2031.
3. **Monthly planner** - defines three must-win outcomes and category actions from August 2026 to December 2027.
4. **Reviews** - records evidence, lessons, finance/life progress, and the next decision.

## Website behavior

- Yearly and monthly notes are editable.
- Checkboxes and text fields save automatically in `localStorage`.
- Data stays in the current browser and is not committed to GitHub.
- Use **Print page** for a quick browser printout.
- Use **Reset browser notes** only when intentionally clearing all local entries.

## Printable planner

The XeLaTeX PDF contains:

- 1 cover page
- 1 icon and visual-roadmap page
- 6 annual planning pages (2026-2031)
- 17 monthly planning pages (Aug 2026-Dec 2027)
- 6 quarterly review pages
- 2 year-end reflection pages

Total: **33 A4 pages**.

## Recommended routine

- Sunday: open the current month and choose three must-wins.
- Daily: select one task that creates visible evidence.
- Month end: record evidence, lesson, and next-month change.
- Quarter end: complete one quarterly review page.
- Year end: complete the annual reflection before changing the next year's plan.
