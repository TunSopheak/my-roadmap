# Detailed Action Plan: August 2026 - December 2027

This document is the execution layer of the five-year strategy. The printable version is in [`roadmap.tex`](./roadmap.tex).

## Weekly minimums before full-time employment

- Technical deep work: **12-15 hours**
- Kibo-RPC during active phases: **6-10 hours**
- English: **30 minutes daily**
- Civil-service study: **30-45 minutes daily**
- Targeted job applications: **3-5 per week**
- Weekly review: **20 minutes every Sunday**

## Weekly minimums after full-time employment

- Work-impact log: update every Friday
- Technical growth: 4-6 hours/week
- English: 20-30 minutes/day
- Civil-service study: 30 minutes/day
- Wedding/relationship planning: one protected block/week
- Recovery: one low-load half-day/week

## Monthly review questions

- What evidence did I create?
- Which task produced the most career progress?
- Which problem repeated?
- Did I speak English, or only consume content?
- Which project must be finished before starting another?
- Is the wedding fund on plan?
- Are health, sleep, or relationships paying the cost of ambition?
- What are the next month's three must-wins?
