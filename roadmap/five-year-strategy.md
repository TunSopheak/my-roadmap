# Five-Year Career & Life Strategy

**Period:** August 2026 - August 2031
**Owner:** Tun Sopheak
**Current context:** Year-4 Computer Science student at RUPP, evening class E8.

## North Star

Become a dependable **Software Engineer** who can take a feature from requirement and design through implementation, testing, deployment, documentation, and support.

## Strategic pillars

1. **Career and technical depth** - Full-Stack Web + Flutter first, then specialize based on real work evidence.
2. **Kibo-RPC excellence** - prepare with reproducible simulations, logs, failure analysis, and robust releases.
3. **English for work** - speaking, technical vocabulary, email writing, project explanation, and interviews.
4. **Portfolio proof** - Love my ស្ទីល, the Year-4 mobile project, and public-safe Kibo-RPC evidence.
5. **Finance and family** - obtain full-time technology income quickly and build a responsible wedding fund through 2027.
6. **Long-term optionality** - study 30-45 minutes daily for the civil-service clerk examination without sacrificing the primary career path.

## Year 1 - Foundation to first role

**August 2026 - July 2027**

- Prepare for Kibo-RPC using an engineering process, not last-minute trial and error.
- Release Love my ស្ទីល v1 and a demonstrable Flutter mobile project.
- Secure a full-time technology role before graduation or within 90 days afterward.
- Become able to introduce myself, explain projects, and complete interviews in English.

**Evidence gate:** two strong portfolio projects, one Kibo case study, targeted applications every week, mock interviews, and at least 80% habit adherence.

## Year 2 - Responsible Software Engineer

**August 2027 - July 2028**

- Own features end-to-end.
- Improve testing, reviews, documentation, logging, and CI/CD.
- Complete the wedding and establish a stable household financial system without damaging debt.
- Keep only one maintained personal project rather than many unfinished projects.

## Year 3 - Mid-level readiness

**August 2028 - July 2029**

- Select a primary specialization: Backend, Mobile, or Full-Stack.
- Handle ambiguity, write design documents, estimate work, and solve production issues.
- Own a module or domain and mentor at least one junior/student.

## Year 4 - Strong mid-level

**August 2029 - July 2030**

- Lead project planning and technical trade-off discussions.
- Deliver a measurable architecture, performance, reliability, security, cost, or developer-experience improvement.
- Connect technical work to business value and stakeholder needs.

## Year 5 - Senior-track Engineer

**August 2030 - August 2031**

- Own a critical system or domain.
- Lead architecture reviews, incident learning, engineering standards, and mentoring.
- Decide the next path using evidence: Senior IC, Engineering Lead, Product Engineer, international opportunity, or founder path.

## Anti-goals

- Do not learn five stacks at the same time.
- Do not start a new project before the current one has a demo, README, and deployment.
- Do not mass-apply without tailoring and learning from outcomes.
- Do not define Senior Engineer by years alone; define it by scope and impact.
- Do not use the wedding fund for lifestyle inflation.
